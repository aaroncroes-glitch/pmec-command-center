# PMEC Role Comparison Validation

The desktop/iPad Control Center now includes a **PM + HR View** navigation item. Its separate comparison page rendered the Project Manager and Human Resources perspectives side by side, with delivery metrics and allocation exceptions on the left, workforce readiness and leave/capacity context on the right, and a shared delivery-handoff strip below.

The comparison page showed three active job orders, 14.5 hours awaiting PM approval, five shared assignments, eight allocation-watch signals, two leave decisions, 354 weekly open-capacity hours, and two workforce availability flags. It also exposed direct actions to open the Project Manager delivery view or HR planning view.

Final validation passed TypeScript and 27 deterministic tests, including dedicated dual-role metric coverage alongside the existing PMEC delivery, capacity forecasting, ESS, and branding suites.
