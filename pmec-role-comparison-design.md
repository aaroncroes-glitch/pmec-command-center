# PMEC Dual-Role Comparison Page

The new desktop/iPad-only **Role Comparison** page places a Project Manager operating lens beside an HR operating lens without merging their permissions. The Project Manager column emphasizes live job-order delivery, hours awaiting approval, delivery assignments, and allocation exceptions. The HR column emphasizes workforce readiness, leave decisions, coverage forecasts, and staffing signals.

Both columns derive their totals from the same existing PMEC control state. A shared-signal strip at the bottom makes the handoff explicit: assignments originate in project delivery, time approval affects employee records, and HR availability planning informs delivery readiness. Each column includes a focused return action that switches the demo role and opens the appropriate Control Center view.
